XRPL EVM is Live — Powered by Grove and Pocket Today marks a major milestone for the XRPL ecosystem: the XRPL EVM Sidechain is officially live on mainnet . Developers can now deploy Ethereum-compatible smart contracts using XRP as gas — backed by fast finality, low fees, and native XRPL liquidity. At Grove , we’re proud to support this launch with robust infrastructure: our Grove Portal and public RPC endpoints for XRPL EVM are available starting today.
